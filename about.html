Our Origin

From Istanbul to the World

Founded in Istanbul with deep roots in Iraq, Mazad Global emerged from a simple realization: the MENA region's 500M+ population deserved a unified digital marketplace that understood its culture, languages, and commerce patterns. We built that platform from the ground up.

Our Mission

One App. Endless Market.

We are building the operating system for commerce in the Arab world — combining real-time auctions, B2B wholesale, C2C trading, AI assistance, digital payments, and creative tools into a single seamless ecosystem called Mazad.

Our Vision

A Borderless Digital Future

By 2028, we aim to operate in 15+ countries, serve 50M+ active users, and process $5B+ in fintech volume — all while staying true to our roots: technology built by, and for, the people of the Middle East.

Headquarters

Istanbul, Turkey — with active operations spanning Iraq, the GCC, and North Africa.

Technology

AI-first architecture powering Yaman Copilot, Mazad Studio & Smart Bidding — all built for regional dialects and commerce patterns.

Fintech

Mazad Cash delivers bank-grade digital wallets, escrow, and multi-currency settlements purpose-built for the Iraqi dinar and regional markets.
